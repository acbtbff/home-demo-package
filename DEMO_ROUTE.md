# Demo Route

1. `/` — entry and navigation.
2. `/floorplan` — inspect and resize the 2D room.
3. `/room` — show the cozy 3D room, desk, office chair, collision/out-of-bounds feedback, and gesture control.
4. `/furniture` — add owned furniture, add wishlist product details, trial place, edit dimensions, and mark purchased.
5. Return to `/room` to confirm the shared RoomDocument and placement state.
